Why PRO-PRESS?

  • Your images stay on your device. Most online compressors quietly send your files to their servers. This one does all the work on your own computer, so there is nothing to upload and nothing to wait on, even on slow internet.
  • Built with WordPress in mind. Resize photos to sensible web sizes, get alt text written for you by AI, and let the tool rename your files so they are search-friendly too.
  • Modern formats included. WebP and AVIF usually cut file sizes in half or more compared to JPEG. If your site is slow, swapping image formats is the easiest win there is.

Frequently asked questions

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Compression, resizing and format conversion all happen inside your browser, so your images never leave your device. The one exception is the AI alt text feature: it sends a single resized copy of one image to an AI service, and only when you tap the Alt button yourself.

Is PRO-PRESS really free?

Yes, completely. No account, no watermark, no daily limit. You can compress a hundred images and download the ZIP without paying anything.

What is the difference between WebP and JPEG?

WebP makes files roughly half the size of JPEG at similar quality, and every major browser supports it now, so it is the sensible default for websites. If you are unsure, convert one image each way and compare them with the preview slider.

How much smaller will my images be?

Most photos shrink by 70 to 90 percent at the default 80% quality setting. If you also resize a 3300px camera photo down to 800px, a 4 MB original can end up around 100 KB.

What is alt text and why does it matter?

Alt text is the short description a screen reader reads out loud, and the one search engines read to understand your picture. Good alt text helps visually impaired visitors and helps your images show up in Google Images. Tap Alt on any file and the tool writes it for you, embeds it in the image, and can rename the file to match.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, in any modern mobile browser, iPhone and Android included. Since your phone does the work itself, you can compress big batches even on a slow connection.
