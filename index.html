Frequently asked questions

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. All compression, resizing and format conversion happens inside your browser on your own device — your images never leave it. The optional AI alt-text feature sends one resized copy of a single image to a secure AI service, and only when you explicitly tap the Alt button.

Is PRO-PRESS really free?

Yes — completely free, with no sign-up, no watermarks and no daily limits. Bulk-compress hundreds of images and download them as a ZIP at no cost.

What is the difference between WebP and JPEG?

WebP is a modern image format that typically produces files 50–80% smaller than JPEG at the same visual quality. Every major browser supports it, which makes WebP the best default choice for websites. Use the built-in preview slider to compare quality yourself before downloading.

How much smaller will my images be?

Most photos shrink by 70–90% at the recommended 80% quality setting. Combine compression with the resize option — for example scaling a 3300px camera photo down to 800px — and a 4 MB original can become a ~100 KB web-ready file.

What is alt text and why does it matter?

Alt text describes an image for screen readers and search engines. It improves accessibility, helps your images rank in Google Images, and WordPress stores it as image metadata. PRO-PRESS writes alt text for you with AI, embeds it into the image file, and can even rename your file to match.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. PRO-PRESS is fully responsive and works in any modern mobile browser, including iPhone and Android. Because processing happens on your device, you don't need a fast connection to compress large batches.
